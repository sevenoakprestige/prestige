## Can a Resident of Algeria Open a UK Company?

Yes.

Residents of Algeria can legally own and operate a UK Limited Company.

You do not need UK citizenship or UK residency to become a company director or shareholder.

Many entrepreneurs in Algeria choose a UK company because it offers international credibility, a recognised corporate structure, and access to global business opportunities.

In many cases, an Algerian entrepreneur can:
- Own 100% of the company shares
- Act as the sole director
- Manage the company remotely
- Serve clients worldwide
- Operate from Algeria while owning a UK company

## Documents Usually Required

To register a UK company, the following documents are typically required:

### Valid Passport
A clear copy of a valid passport.

### Proof of Residential Address
Examples may include:
- Utility bill
- Bank statement
- Government correspondence

### Company Information
Information such as:
- Proposed company name
- Business activity
- Director details
- Shareholder details

Additional verification requirements may apply depending on individual circumstances.
