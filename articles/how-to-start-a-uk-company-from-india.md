# How to Start a UK Company from India

Indian entrepreneurs are rapidly expanding into global markets, especially in sectors such as SaaS, IT services, software development, digital agencies, consulting, import-export, and online businesses.

A UK Limited Company is one of the most preferred structures for Indian founders who want to operate internationally, build global credibility, and access overseas clients.

The UK allows non-residents to register and own a company remotely, making it a strong jurisdiction for international business expansion.

## Can an Indian Resident Open a UK Company?

Yes.

Indian residents can legally register and own a UK Limited Company.

You do not need UK residency or citizenship.

Most Indian founders can:
- Own 100% shares
- Be sole director
- Operate remotely from India
- Serve global clients
- Build international business structure
