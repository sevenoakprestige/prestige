## What Is a Registered Office Address?

Every UK limited company must maintain a Registered Office Address.

This address appears on the public Companies House register and is used for official correspondence from government bodies and regulatory authorities. A professional Registered Office Address helps ensure important statutory documents are received and managed efficiently.

## What Is a Director Service Address?

A Director Service Address allows directors to use a professional correspondence address instead of displaying their residential address on the public register where applicable.

Benefits include:
- Greater privacy
- Professional presentation
- Separation of personal and business information
- Enhanced credibility

Many international entrepreneurs choose this option to maintain a professional business structure.

## What Is a London Business Address?

### Do I need to visit the United Kingdom?

### What documents will I receive?
Standard company formation documents typically include:
