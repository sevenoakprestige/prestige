## SECTION 8 — WHAT DOCUMENTS DO I NEED?
- **H2:** Documents Required From Indian Founders
- **Create a two-column checklist:**
  - **Passport / Identity Document:** A valid accepted identity document for the director, shareholder and relevant PSC.
  - **Proof of Residential Address:** Recent evidence of the founder’s genuine Indian residential address where required for KYC/compliance. Examples may include accepted bank statement, utility bill, government-issued address document (depending on the relevant verification process).
  - **Business Activity Information:** We need to understand what the company will do; products/services; target customers; countries of operation; expected business model.
  - **Director / Shareholder Details:** Including full legal name; date of birth; nationality; residential address; shareholding percentage; ownership/control information.
  - **Company Details:** proposed company name; business activity; email; share structure; proposed SIC codes.
  - **Additional Compliance Documents:** Depending on the activity, banking, VAT or risk profile, additional evidence may include source of funds; source of wealth; contracts; website; supplier/customer information; existing company documents.
- **Important line:** Requirements depend on the individual founder, selected service and business activity. We will tell you exactly what is required before submission.
