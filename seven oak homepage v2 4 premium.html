Frequently asked questions

Questions worth answering before you order.

Straightforward answers on registration, pricing, addresses and eligibility.

How much does it cost to form a UK limited company?

Seven Oak formation packages cost £199 (Starter), £299 (Prestige) and £399 (Elite). Each price includes the £100 Companies House digital incorporation fee. Address renewals after year one are published: registered office £59/year, director service address £35/year, virtual business address £150/year.

Can a non-UK resident register a UK limited company?

Yes. UK residence is not generally required to own or direct a UK private limited company. Applicants must meet identity, address and eligibility requirements.

Can a UK resident use your formation packages?

Yes. We support UK-based founders as well as international entrepreneurs. Starter is designed for those who already have an eligible UK statutory address.

Is the Companies House incorporation fee included?

Yes. The standard £100 digital incorporation fee is included in our Starter, Prestige and Elite formation prices.

Do I need a UK address to form a company?

Yes. Every UK company needs a registered office address in the UK. Directors also need a service address, which can be a business address and not the home address. Prestige and Elite include both for the first 12 months. PO Boxes are not accepted as a registered office.

Which package includes a London address?

Prestige and Elite include registered office and director service address services for the first 12 months. Elite also includes a virtual business address for the first 12 months, subject to the service terms.

Do directors need to verify their identity with Companies House?

Yes. Companies House identity verification applies to directors and people with significant control. Seven Oak can help you through the process, and Elite includes assistance for one director. Verification is completed directly with Companies House. Seven Oak is not an authorised corporate service provider (ACSP) and does not verify identities on Companies House's behalf.

How does Elite support a UK business banking application?

Elite helps you prepare your business profile, supporting documents and application information for suitable banking or payment providers. Each provider assesses applications independently under its own eligibility and compliance criteria.

What happens after the first year?

Companies must meet ongoing filing and tax obligations, including accounts, a Corporation Tax return and a Confirmation Statement. Address services renew at the published rates. Accounting support is a separate service, from £699 a year.
